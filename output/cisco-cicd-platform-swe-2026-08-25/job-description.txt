Meet the team:
At Cisco, our intuitive platform enables organizations of all sizes to deliver customer and employee experiences at scale. Our newly formed team is part of Engineering Systems within Cisco Collaboration, and we are hiring engineers to help shape its future. Our mission is to improve the developer experience by building a modern CI/CD and release automation platform for a large-scale enterprise environment. Engineering teams across the organization will use this platform to build, test, publish, and deploy software through a unified, Git-driven workflow—with configuration managed in Git, secrets secured in Vault, and automated deployments to Kubernetes or VM-based environments. This team will play a key role in simplifying delivery workflows, increasing engineering productivity, and enabling reliable, secure, and consistent software releases at scale.

Role: Full Stack Software Engineer on the CI/CD Platform team. Use AI-assisted development tools as a core part of how you deliver software. Collaborate with product, backend, DevOps, and QA engineers to architect, design, and build a full-stack application for pipeline orchestration, release control, deployment visibility, audit logging, and enterprise integrations.

- Use AI-assisted development tools (e.g., Cursor, Codex, or equivalent) effectively across the full Agile SDLC.
- Build a full-stack application for a new CI/CD platform: pipeline state, release workflows, deployment visibility, and operational control surfaces.
- Contribute to AI-native platform capabilities, including MCP servers and AI agents that detect deployment failures, recommend or automate rollbacks, and guide operators through troubleshooting.
- Solve complicated architecture problems to build scalable and extendable systems.

- Write unit tests and end-to-end tests for critical CI/CD flows—from trigger through build, publish, deploy, and verify.
- Deploy and operate platform services in production using CI/CD, GitOps, Kubernetes, and cloud infrastructure.
- Optimize platform performance and reliability—profiling services, tuning databases and APIs, and improving scalability under production load.
- Take complete ownership from conception to production release.
